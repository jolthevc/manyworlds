# Creator aesthetics: looking aspirational, not boring (2026-11-24)

**Evidence quality:** no public study measures the face vs non-face share in creator feeds. Platform numbers come from Metricool, Buffer and CreatorIQ. Shot and sequencing rules come from practitioner guides that agree with each other. The percentages under "What to encode" are **starting priors for `brain/predictions.py`**, not findings.

## 1. Post mix and shot types

- **Format economics (2026).** Metricool's 2026 study (24.4M posts, ~375k accounts) found that carousels earn about 9x the saves of single images. Reels reach about 29% more people than carousels. Engagement rate is Reels 4.30%, carousels 3.08%, other formats 1.99%. Single-image engagement fell 46% year on year. https://metricool.com/press-release-instagram-study-2026/ , https://t3n.de/news/instagram-formate-beste-performance-metricool-studie-1751388/ . Buffer (52M posts): carousel engagement by reach 6.90% vs 3.31% for Reels. Implication: almost no single photos; carousels carry saves and identity, Reels carry reach.
- **Ranking signals.** Mosseri names watch time, likes per reach and **sends per reach** as the heaviest signals. Originality is up-ranked, and reposts are demoted on photos and carousels too since 2026. https://buffer.com/resources/instagram-algorithms/ , https://petapixel.com/2026/04/30/new-instagram-policies-target-reposted-content/ , https://techcrunch.com/2024/04/30/instagram-is-updating-its-ranking-systems-to-surface-more-content-from-smaller-original-creators
- **Faceless and partial shots are the connective tissue.** Guides recommend hands, shoes, jewellery, fabric, reflections, back-turned and silhouette shots between full-outfit or face posts, and a drink to give the hands something to do. https://www.iconosquare.com/blog/10-ideas-for-faceless-instagram-reels , https://plannthat.com/10-faceless-instagram-reels-ideas , https://www.shopify.com/au/blog/aesthetic-instagram , https://www.zebracat.ai/post/faceless-reels
- **The 2025-26 look is "unedited yet curated".** It means camera-roll batches, blur and motion shots, grain, and on-camera **flash** in the digicam style ("bright flash, wide angles, strong contrast"). This replaced the hyper-saturated feeds of the 2016 era. https://later.com/blog/instagram-aesthetic-trends/ , https://www.canon.co.uk/view/genz-digicams/ , https://www.diyphotography.net/gen-z-photography-trends/ , https://www.vice.com/en/article/why-everyones-instagram-feed-looks-so-ugly-right-now/ , https://dailynorthwestern.com/2026/03/12/ae/turning-your-feed-fabulous-how-to-achieve-a-gen-z-approved-instagram/

Named shot types (44, with framing and light) are under "What to encode" (a).

## 2. A consistent vibe while wardrobe and places vary

- **The palette is the invariant, not the clothes.** 3-5 signature colours (not all in every post); the same preset, contrast and light, white balance fine-tuned per image; checked at thumbnail size. https://www.socialpilot.co/instagram-marketing/how-to-make-instagram-aesthetic , https://www.bontraveler.com/how-to-edit-photos-for-a-consistent-instagram-feed/ , https://later.com/blog/instagram-grid/ , https://plannthat.com/aesthetic-image-editing-tips
- **Grade families seen in 2025-26** (Later): unedited/raw; pink-hue grain; "gloomy and grainy" (muted, minimal, editorial, "bright colors need not apply"); blurred motion. https://later.com/blog/instagram-aesthetic-trends/
- **Repeating outfits is now positive.** Re-wearing is framed as personal style and under-consumption; "signature pieces" are their own genre. https://www.brandwatch.com/blog/fashion-industry-trends/ , https://www.whowhatwear.com/repeat-clothing-items , https://thelookonline.dcu.ie/2025/11/07/outfit-repeater-why-wearing-it-twice-is-the-new-power-move/ .
- **Character from backstory and everyday specifics.** The one AI creator whose sponsored engagement beat human averages (Aitana Lopez: Olaplex 8.9%, Victoria's Secret 6.5%) is built around consistent specifics: the food she eats, the bands she listens to, the games she plays. https://www.creatoriq.com/blog/ai-influencers-human-influencers-engagement , https://www.euronews.com/2024/12/27/meet-the-first-spanish-ai-model-earning-up-to-10000-per-month
- **Anchor vs drift (inference, matching `sets.py`).** Talking, GRWM and podcast content uses 1-2 fixed home sets (same chair, mirror, light). Aesthetic and travel posts drift, held together by palette and mood. https://www.bellanaija.com/2026/02/how-to-curate-an-insta-dump-that-actually-hits/

## 3. Grid and carousel craft

- **Ratio.** In 2025 the grid moved from 1:1 to tall previews: 4:5 (1080x1350) for images and carousels, 3:4 for Reel covers. Some sources say 3:4 for all thumbnails. Keep faces and text in the centre 1080x1080, which survives both crops. The first slide's ratio locks the whole carousel. https://buffer.com/resources/instagram-grid/ , https://stan.store/blog/instagram-post-size-guide-2026/ , https://metricool.com/instagram-design/
- **Photo dump structure** (Bella Naija 2026, Later, Hootsuite, Adobe). Shoot a pool of 20-30 frames. Post 7-10 slides, never in camera-roll order.
  1. Cover: the strongest single image, a striking portrait or outfit shot (the hook, which also appears on the grid).
  2. Context: a wide outfit frame, mirror shot or location.
  3. Personality: a candid laugh, a motion blur, a behind-the-scenes moment.
  4. Middle: details (accessories, beauty close-up, shoes, well-lit food).
  5. Tail: "controlled chaos" (night flash, scenery, a screenshot, an outfit alternate).
  https://www.bellanaija.com/2026/02/how-to-curate-an-insta-dump-that-actually-hits/ , https://later.com/blog/instagram-photo-dump-trend/ , https://blog.hootsuite.com/photo-dump/ , https://www.adobe.com/express/learn/blog/photo-dump
- **Face ratio (our prior):** the face is clear on the cover plus 1-2 slides; the rest partial or none. A caption question drew ~37% more comments (Metricool via https://www.emarketer.com/content/carousels-questions-drive-instagram-engagement ).

## 4. Educational carousels

- **Thin evidence:** no study isolates slide count; consensus is 7-10 of a possible 20 (ContentStudio). https://contentstudio.io/blog/instagram-carousel . Optimise for saves.
- **Design system** (practitioner consensus):
  - One font pairing and a small colour set, reused on every slide (https://zoviz.com/blog/instagram-carousel-design-tips-saves-shares ; https://www.canva.com/design-school/resources/how-to-create-instagram-carousels-worth-swiping-for).
  - Cover headline of 8 words or fewer, at least 60 px on a 1080 canvas, still readable when zoomed to 25% (thumbnail test). https://carouselli.com/blog/instagram-carousel-cover-image
  - Headline 48-72 pt equivalent, body 24-36 pt, with the headline taking 40-60% of the cover. https://www.lovart.ai/it/blog/02-cluster-instagram-carousel
  - Fixed headline size bands, and a rule for overflowing text instead of free scaling (https://orshot.com/templates/2111).
- **Video in carousels.** Short video slides ("video swipies") are a 2025-26 scroll-stopper: https://social.colostate.edu/strategy/beyond-the-carousel-why-video-swipies-stop-the-scroll/

## 5. Reels for aesthetic creators

- **Pacing.**
  - Day-in-the-life: 1-3 s per clip for upbeat edits, 2-4 s for calm ones, a visual change every 2-4 s after the hook, in chronological order through the day. https://picsart.com/blog/day-in-my-life-trend/ , https://blitzcutai.com/blog/lifestyle-reel-hooks-transitions-2026 , https://spliceapp.com/blog/mastering-travel-day-pacing-for-engaging-reels/
  - Talking or tutorial: a cut every 1.5-3 s. Trend or transition edits: under 1 s.
  - Slow living runs longer, with slow motion "to absorb details" (Getty VisualGPS). https://emergencycreative.squarespace.com/blog/why-i-refuse-to-cut-every-4-seconds-the-case-for-slow-and-intentional-editing
- **Structures.** Day-in-the-life (morning, outing, evening); slow living (ambient sound, long holds, hands doing tasks); POV text over B-roll (clips + custom text overlay + atmospheric filter, per Later). A subtle zoom moves static shots.
- **Cinematic vs phone-native.** The trend favours phone-native: flash, grain, imperfect framing, raw-but-curated (Later, Canon, Vice). "Cinematic AI edits" are listed as a 2026 trend, but they read as AI. https://contentstudio.io/blog/trending-visuals-for-instagram/ . Use cinematic sparingly, for travel or establishing shots.

## 6. Why AI creators look fake or boring, and what works

- **Data.** CreatorIQ: AI creators "almost uniformly underperform" on sponsored engagement (Miquela 0.6% vs a 3.6% brand average; Imma 0.3% vs 4.6%). Aitana Lopez is the exception (realism, imperfection, spontaneity). Causes: uncanny valley, disclosure scepticism, **sameness** (polished, conventionally attractive, culturally neutral looks converge). https://www.creatoriq.com/blog/ai-influencers-human-influencers-engagement
  - HypeAuditor (secondary): virtual influencers higher organic engagement (2.84% vs 1.72%), 2.7x lower sponsored. https://dev.to/rawveg/virtual-stars-real-losses-26f9 . 15% of consumers trust them highly. https://betanews.com/2025/09/11/brands-are-increasingly-testing-ai-influencers-but-trust-in-them-is-low/ . More realism can tip into the uncanny valley. https://bura.brunel.ac.uk/bitstream/2438/34081/3/FullText.pdf
- **Visual tells.**
  - Hands and fingers, and scrambled text on signs and labels. https://clutch.co/resources/signs-of-ai-generated-photos , https://euronews.com/next/2025/02/16/ai-slop-is-flooding-the-internet-this-is-how-can-you-tell-if-an-image-is-artificially-gene
  - AI faces "arrive at the camera fully and exactly", with no slightly-off frame. https://www.designrush.com/best-designs/video/trends/ai-influencers-when-brands-design-people
  - Behavioural tells: rigid posting windows. https://aiweekly.co/alerts/behavioral-patterns-now-expose-ai-influencer-accounts
- **Platform (Sep 2026).** An "AI-generated profile" label is required, and unlabeled AI-person accounts get limited reach. Users dislike finding out later; our openly-AI stance is aligned. https://petapixel.com/2026/09/01/instagram-is-waking-up-to-the-fact-that-people-dont-like-being-tricked-by-ai-creators/ , https://www.digitaltrends.com/social-media/instagram-will-limit-reach-for-ai-influencers-who-skip-ai-generated-label-on-their-profile/
- **Boring = sameness** (inference): every post a centred, smiling face-to-camera portrait in the same light. **Aspirational =** the world around the person, partial shots viewers project into, specific tastes, imperfection.

### (b) Consistency vs variety rules

- **Invariants per creator** (profile; checked at review): palette (3-5 hex); one grade family (raw, warm-film, pink-grain, gloomy-grainy, flash-digicam) plus one night alternate; 2-3 signature items in >=30% of posts showing the creator; 1-2 anchor sets for talking/GRWM/podcast; 3-5 recurring motifs; specific tastes (foods, places, music) reused in captions and props.
- **Variety:** no shot type in consecutive slides or more than twice per carousel; alternate wide/close; no repeated pose; >=3 shot categories per carousel; outfits may repeat ("re-wear") after 7+ days with new styling; travel in arcs (one trip per 4-6 weeks over 4-8 posts) with home weeks between; jittered posting times.
- **Imperfection budget:** each carousel includes at least one "imperfect" frame (motion blur, flash, off-centre crop, grain). Gaze goes off-camera in at least half of the face shots.
- **Anti-tells:** hands only where the hand gate passes; no generated legible text (overlays only); centred face-to-camera smile capped at 1 per carousel and 1 in 3 grid posts.

### (c) Carousel template set

- **Spec:** 1080x1350, safe centre 1080x1080, margins >=90 px; per creator one headline and one body font (OFL, `fonts/`), 2 brand colours plus a neutral, small handle mark bottom-left; cover headline 72-110 px and <=8 words (passes the 25% zoom test), slide headline 56-72 px, body 32-40 px and <=30 words; 7-10 slides (cap 12).
- **Templates:**
  1. `cover`: a creator or aesthetic photo with a headline over a scrim. Formula: number + outcome, or tension ("5 things I stopped buying"; "the 10-minute Sunday reset"). Swipe cue at the edge.
  2. `list_item`: a big numeral, a short heading, one line, a small supporting photo or detail shot.
  3. `step`: step n/N progress, an action verb heading, a photo of the hands doing it.
  4. `quote`: a large pull quote in the creator's voice, on a plain colour.
  5. `comparison`: split-screen (this/that, before/after, do/don't), with 2 labels.
  6. `recap`: a checklist of all points (this is the slide people save).
  7. `cta`: an ask to save or send ("send this to the friend who..."), a question for comments, and the disclosure line where required.
- **Mix:** creator on the cover (face yes/partial), a photo on >=50% of inner slides (a lifestyle account, not a deck), optionally one video slide.
- **Photo dump template:** `cover(face) → context(wide/mirror) → personality(candid/motion) → 3-4 details → 1-3 tail (night/scenery/object)`, 7-10 slides, face clearly visible on 2-3.
